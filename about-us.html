About us

Welcome to hiiscript.com were we help beginners and professionals dive deep into Html, css and javascipt for better understanding. We make learning simple and fun with our simple quizes to help you understand more about what you are learning. Our site helps everybody with Html, Css and Javascript whether you are a beginner looking to learn how to code or a professional who want to improve their skills. We believe that anybody can learn how to code and will eventually know how to code without being good at maths or going to school to learn how to code.


We are a team of passionate web developers and designers dedicated to making coding enjoyable. Our goal is to provide free web development tools, free resources and high-quality information for anyone looking to learn or improve their skills. We offer simple to understand structured Html, Css and Javascript lessons that cover all concepts of those three powerful languages.

Hiiscript is a free website

We make our site free for every person to use because we believe that coding should be accessible, simple and completely free for everyone. That is why we created this site to offer top-notch easy to follow tutorials, examples, tools and resources completely free for people who want to learn or improve their html, css and javascipt skills.

Our future goals

Our future goals is to grow this site with more tools, resources, lessons and content to improve our website to make learning more enjoyable. We promise to keep our website updated because we are committed to provide high-quality free learning, web development tools and resources for learning this skills.
